PHASE 1 – BRAINSTORMING & IDEATION PHASE

Project Title:
ComicCraft – AI Comic Story Creator

Project Idea:

ComicCraft is an AI-powered comic story creation platform that helps users transform their creative ideas into structured comic stories. The system uses Artificial Intelligence to generate story content and supports the creation of comic panels.

Problem Statement:

Creating a complete comic manually requires significant time, creativity, writing skills, and artistic effort. Beginners may find it difficult to convert a simple story idea into a structured comic with multiple scenes and characters.

Objectives:

1. To generate comic stories from user-provided ideas.
2. To create structured comic panels.
3. To provide different story tones and art styles.
4. To reduce the time required to create a comic.
5. To provide an easy-to-use interface.
6. To support users in creating AI-assisted comic stories.

Proposed Solution:

ComicCraft provides an AI-assisted platform where users can enter a story idea, character, setting, story tone, art style, and number of panels. The system processes the input using AI and generates a structured comic story.

Expected Outcome:

The expected outcome is an easy-to-use AI-based application that helps users create structured comic stories quickly and efficiently.
